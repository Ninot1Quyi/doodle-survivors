CURRENT DISTRIBUTION AND PROCESSING
63 weapon keys use 67 OGG launch files, including two additional real page turns
and two additional real whip takes. Matching physical actions may share suitable
recordings. Single-source launch processing: silence trim, mono 44.1 kHz,
material EQ, mild compression, level alignment and edge fades. No new synthesis,
layering or pitch shift is used in that offline launch pass. Some source files
are already authored electronic/musical effects. Per-file authors, source/output
hashes and exact processing: WEAPON-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json; weapons/CREDITS.md.

Firecracker/festival use the real fireworks excerpt 1.14–1.54 s, gain 0.6, mono,
boundary fades and OGG encoding. Three independent BigSoundBank whip takes are
used instead of re-labeling a knife clip. Cuts now use artisticdude recorded air swishes; defeats use Dustyroom short
rounded pops rather than pumpkin/crunch. The retained electrical loop is
unchanged. See COMBAT-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json and COMBAT-CREDITS.md. Contacts
use Kenney generic/light-glass taps and Dustyroom short pops, with linear EQ,
gain and cosine edge fades; see contacts/PROVENANCE.json and contacts/CREDITS.txt.
Weapon launches and contacts have separate voice budgets; sustained beam loops
follow the active attack lifetime. This source notice does not claim human
listening acceptance from decoding, waveform or automated game checks.

Reward/collection cues: Kenney Music Jingles jingles_PIZZI10 (short rising
upgrade phrase), jingles_PIZZI08 (upward gift phrase and a single upper-note
pickup excerpt), linear EQ/gain/cosine fades; REWARD-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json.
PIZZI07, highUp and pepSound3 are no longer the inspiration/gift/soft-pickup
files. This does not remove unrelated weapon/chest uses of their source pack. Chest reels use
separate authored slot/reveal/jackpot cues from Kenney and Dustyroom; provenance
is in WEAPON-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json.

Legacy Kenney original/Foley/warm variants remain distributed. Their historical
trim/EQ/fade/normalization, small resampling pitch changes and premixes are in
MIX-PROVENANCE.json and WARM-IMPACT-PROVENANCE.json. These historical treatments
must not be confused with the single-source stage14 launch palette.
