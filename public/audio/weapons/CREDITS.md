# Weapon launch audio credits

All weapon launch samples are derived from authored CC0 1.0 sources. Full license: [CC0-1.0.txt](CC0-1.0.txt). Attribution is voluntary; original source and output SHA-256 values are retained in [WEAPON-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json](../WEAPON-AUDIO-PROVENANCE.json).

- Kenney: [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), [Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio), [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds).
- Dustyroom: [Free Casual Game Sounds](https://dustyroom.com/free-casual-game-sounds/).
- artisticdude: [Swishes Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack); recorded clothes-hanger and stick swishes.
- rubberduck: [80 CC0 RPG SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx); short blades, fire spells, stone/gem and metal sounds.
- StarNinjas: [20 Sword Sound Effects — Attacks and Clashes](https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes); blade movements.
- Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank: [Complete whip #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/complete-whip-1-s2961.html), [Complete whip #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/complete-whip-2-s2962.html), [Whip crack #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/whip-crack-1-s2949.html). Files whip.ogg / whip2.ogg / whip3.ogg contain independent takes.
- Rudmer_Rotteveel: [Whistle and Explosion Single_Firework](https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/336008/). Existing firecracker.ogg remains byte-identical; festival.ogg deliberately shares this real fireworks source.

Processing preserves one source per launch cue: mono conversion, silence trim, material-specific high-pass, 7.2 kHz low-pass, mild dynamic compression, level alignment and short edge fades. No synthesis, sound layering or artificial pitch variation. Source recordings may themselves be authored electronic effects where the weapon is electrical or musical.

Voice matching and waveform checks are recorded in [the audit](../../../docs/weapon-timbre-audit.md). They do not certify subjective listening approval.
